## Shared authoring control

`fringeStyle` is an optional anatomy-only setting with exactly `swept` and
`center_part`. Explicit values, including `swept`, require4.10.10. Omission adds
no default field, preserves old request bytes and keeps the earlier feature
floors. Manual Human acting exposes Hair fringe when Figure style is
`anatomy_sketch`; selecting Swept omits the field. Ordinary MCP edit, retained
human settings and nested story/correction routes share the strict schema and
version admission. No canonical action or hosted model was added.

Center part replaces the old upward/swept tuft recipe with two sets of curved
closed locks. Their centerlines follow the existing cranial envelope, their
finite longitudinal/radial sections have actual thickness, and view-facing
selection avoids drawing a far frontal lock through the back of the skull.
The cap, locks and flow details become ordinary editable native paths. These
are original generic construction guides, not recovered artist strokes, source
pixels, fitted actor identity or guaranteed unseen views.

The rear curtain is a separate authored material. At front/side cameras it is
composed before the owner's body/neck; at rear cameras it remains nearer than
the body. It is excluded from the body skin union even if its selected colour
matches the skin colour. This is a view-aware composition policy, not general
per-pixel3D hair simulation.
